# within Support

## English

### Requirements

iPhone 17 Pro, iPhone 17 Pro Max, iPhone 18 Pro or iPhone 18 Pro Max with iOS 27, and about 12 GB of free storage
for the model. On other iPhones you can view and manage synced chats, but the model does not run.

### Downloading the model

- Open within and tap **Download model**. The download is about 10.5 GB; Wi-Fi is recommended.
- To download over cellular, turn on **Allow cellular downloads** first. It is off by default.
- The download continues in the background and resumes where it stopped. You can pause and resume it at any time.
- If it fails, tap **Try again**. Files already downloaded are kept.

### Using within offline

After the model is downloaded, within works without a network connection, including in airplane mode.

### Temporary chats

On an empty chat, tap the dashed bubble in the top right. Nothing in a temporary chat is saved, and it clears when you
leave it or close the app.

### Instructions

In Settings, **How should within respond?** tells within your preferred language, tone or style (up to 800
characters). Changes apply to the next reply.

### iCloud sync

Turn on **Sync with iCloud** in Settings to keep chats, instructions and generation settings in your private iCloud
database across your devices. It is off by default. Turning it off stops syncing; chats already in iCloud stay there
until you delete them.

### Deleting

- **One chat:** in Chats, swipe left on it or long-press it and choose Delete.
- **Several chats:** long-press a chat, choose **Select**, pick the chats, then tap Delete.
- **Clear chats from this iPhone** (Settings) removes chats from this iPhone only; chats in iCloud stay.
- **Delete all chats** (Settings) deletes your chats on this iPhone and in iCloud.
- **The model:** in the Model screen, tap **Delete model files**. Your chats stay.

Every delete asks you to confirm first.

### Contact

Email within@royomni.com. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version.

## 简体中文

### 设备要求

iPhone 17 Pro、iPhone 17 Pro Max、iPhone 18 Pro 或 iPhone 18 Pro Max，系统为 iOS 27，并为模型预留约 12 GB 可用空间。
在其他 iPhone 上可以查看和管理已同步的聊天，但无法运行模型。

### 下载模型

- 打开 within，轻点**下载模型**。下载约 10.5 GB，建议使用 Wi-Fi。
- 如需使用蜂窝网络下载，请先打开**允许使用蜂窝网络下载**（默认关闭）。
- 下载会在后台继续，中断后从断点续传，可以随时暂停和继续。
- 如果下载失败，轻点**重试**，已下载的文件会保留。

### 离线使用

模型下载完成后，within 无需联网即可使用，飞行模式下也可以。

### 临时聊天

在空白聊天中，轻点右上角的虚线气泡。临时聊天的内容不会保存，离开或关闭应用后即清除。

### 指令

在「设置」的**你希望 within 怎样回复？**中，可以告诉 within 你偏好的语言、语气或风格（最多 800 字），修改会用于下一次回复。

### iCloud 同步

在「设置」中打开**通过 iCloud 同步**，聊天、指令和生成设置会保存在你的 iCloud 私有数据库中，并在你的设备间同步。同步默认关闭。
关闭同步只会停止同步，已在 iCloud 中的聊天会保留，直到你删除。

### 删除

- **单个聊天**：在「聊天」列表中左滑，或长按后选择删除。
- **多个聊天**：长按一个聊天，选择**选择**，勾选要删除的聊天后轻点删除。
- 「设置」中的**清除本机聊天**只删除本机上的聊天，iCloud 中的聊天会保留。
- 「设置」中的**删除全部聊天**会删除本机和 iCloud 中的聊天。
- **模型**：在模型页面轻点**删除模型文件**，聊天会保留。

每次删除前都会请你确认。

### 联系我们

请发邮件至 within@royomni.com，并注明你的 iPhone 型号和 iOS 版本。
