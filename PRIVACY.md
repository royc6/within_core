# within Privacy Policy

[English](#english) · [简体中文](#简体中文)

## English

**Effective date:** October 1, 2026

within is an AI chat app that generates replies on your iPhone. This policy explains what data the
app handles and where it goes.

### Summary

- Your prompts and replies are generated on your iPhone. They are never sent to a remote AI service.
- within has no account, no ads, no usage analytics, no tracking and no third-party SDKs that
  collect data.
- The developer does not operate a server and does not receive your chats or any other personal
  data.

### Data that stays on your iPhone

Chats, custom instructions, generation settings and the downloaded model are stored on your iPhone.
Model files and inference caches are excluded from device backups. The app keeps a small local log
about model downloads and loading (for example, file names, timings and token counts). The log
contains no chat text and is never sent anywhere.

### When the app uses the network

within connects to the network only in these cases:

1. **Model download, when you start it.** The model files are downloaded from Hugging Face
   (huggingface.co) and the content delivery networks it uses. Like any web server, they receive
   ordinary connection information such as your IP address. Their handling of that information is
   governed by the Hugging Face privacy policy. Cellular downloads are off until you turn them on.
2. **iCloud sync, if you turn it on.** Sync is off by default. When it is on, your chats, custom
   instructions and generation settings are stored by Apple in the private CloudKit database of your
   Apple Account and synced to your other devices signed in to the same account. The developer cannot
   read this data. Apple's handling of iCloud data is governed by Apple's privacy policy.
3. **Links you choose to open**, such as the model license or download source.

Inference runs offline. You can use within in airplane mode after the model is downloaded.

### Your choices

- **Turn off Sync with iCloud** in Settings. Data already in iCloud stays there until you delete it.
- **Clear chats from this phone** in Settings removes chats from this iPhone only. Sync stays as it
  is and your iCloud copy is not changed; Sync now or turning sync on brings those chats back. Chats
  that are not in iCloud can't be recovered.
- **Delete all chats** in Settings deletes your chats on this iPhone and in iCloud. With sync on,
  the deletion also reaches your other devices.
- **Delete the model** in the Model screen, or delete the app to remove all of its local data.

### Children

within is rated 18+ and is not directed to children.

### Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.

### Contact

within@royomni.com

## 简体中文

**生效日期：** 2026 年 10 月 1 日

within 是一款在你的 iPhone 上生成回复的 AI 聊天应用。本政策说明应用处理哪些数据、数据去向何处。

### 概要

- 你的提问和回复都在你的 iPhone 上生成，从不发送到任何远程 AI 服务。
- within 没有账号、广告、使用分析、跟踪，也不含任何收集数据的第三方 SDK。
- 开发者不运营服务器，不会收到你的聊天或任何其他个人数据。

### 留在 iPhone 上的数据

聊天、自定义指令、生成设置和已下载的模型都保存在你的 iPhone 上；模型文件和推理缓存不进入设备备份。应用在本机保留一份关于模型下载与加载的简短日志（例如文件名、耗时、token 数），不含聊天内容，也不会发送到任何地方。

### 应用何时联网

within 只在以下情况联网：

1. **你主动开始的模型下载。** 模型文件从 Hugging Face（huggingface.co）及其使用的内容分发网络下载。与任何网站一样，它们会收到你的 IP 地址等常规连接信息，其处理方式适用 Hugging Face 的隐私政策。蜂窝网络下载默认关闭，需你手动开启。
2. **你开启的 iCloud 同步。** 同步默认关闭。开启后，你的聊天、自定义指令和生成设置由 Apple 存放在你 Apple 账户的 CloudKit 私有数据库中，并同步到登录同一账户的其他设备。开发者无法读取这些数据；Apple 对 iCloud 数据的处理适用 Apple 的隐私政策。
3. **你主动打开的链接**，例如模型许可或下载来源。

推理全程离线。模型下载完成后，可以在飞行模式下使用 within。

### 你的选择

- 在「设置」中关闭**通过 iCloud 同步**。已在 iCloud 中的数据会保留，直到你删除。
- 「设置」中的**清除本机聊天**只删除本机上的聊天：同步开关不变，iCloud 中的副本不受影响；点「立即同步」或重新打开同步后，这些聊天会回来。不在 iCloud 中的聊天无法恢复。
- 「设置」中的**删除全部聊天**会删除本机和 iCloud 中的聊天；同步开启时，删除也会同步到你的其他设备。
- 在模型页面**删除模型**，或删除应用以移除其全部本地数据。

### 儿童

within 的年龄分级为 18+，不面向儿童。

### 变更

本政策如有变更，新版本将发布在本地址并更新生效日期。

### 联系方式

within@royomni.com
